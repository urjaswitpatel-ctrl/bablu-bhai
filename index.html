The face you pull like taffy

This is not an animation on rails. Bablu Bhai is a real-time 3D character who watches your cursor, answers every poke, and loses his temper on a schedule of his own.

The Taffy Pull

Grab near his nose or cheeks and drag - his whole face stretches after your cursor and snaps back with a wobble.

The Wink

Hover an eye and he gives you the slow wink, like you two share a secret.

The Scowl

Linger on his forehead and the eyebrows come down. He has opinions about your manners.

The Gasp

Hover his mouth and he gasps - scandalised that you would do such a thing.

The Twirl

Brush the moustache and he twirls it himself. Pride, maintained on the hour.

The Chai Break

Keep poking and he steams, shakes, and explodes into a chai break. He always comes back. He always remembers.

The Thursday Sketch

One bad idea and one good face, every Thursday. Leave, and he takes it personally.
